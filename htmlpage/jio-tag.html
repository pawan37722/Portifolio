When your coding assistant (like Cursor) runs out of tokens, generate a free Groq API key and keep coding.

Why I made this

  • Existing GeoTag apps use editable watermarks, so they can't be trusted for verification.
  • My app captures a photo/video with location and timestamp, generates a unique URL, and stores the media securely.
  • Metadata and media can't be edited, which makes verification tamper-proof.
  • Goal: prevent scams like the 2025 Khandwa rainwater harvesting case, where AI-generated images and a single photo reused many times were used as proof.
  • Tech stack: React, Node.js/Express.js, Multer, PostgreSQL, Google Maps API.
